Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains when BAGO PLATFORM offers a refund for a shipment booked through sendwithbago.com or the Bago app. A payment confirmation alone does not mean that the traveller has collected your item.

When you are eligible for a refund

You are eligible for a refund when the shipment did not take place and the traveller cancelled the booking before collecting or taking possession of your item.

You are also eligible when a dispute review confirms that the traveller never collected or took possession of your item and the shipment did not take place. Opening a dispute does not itself approve a refund; Bago first reviews the booking and the available evidence.

What this policy does not cover

A sender changing their mind is not, by itself, one of the refund conditions above. Once the traveller has collected the item, an automatic refund is not available under this policy. Delivery, loss, damage or other issues after collection should be reported to Bago for a separate dispute or insurance review where applicable.

This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.

How to request a refund or raise a dispute

Contact us at send@sendwithbago.com, call +234 808 100 8086 or +34 632 466 227, or open a support request in the Bago app or website. Include your booking or tracking number, the email used for the booking, your payment reference, and an explanation of the cancellation or non-collection.

For a dispute, include the relevant messages, cancellation notice, pickup arrangements and any other evidence showing that the traveller did not take the item. Do not send your full card number, CVV, password or one-time payment codes.

Review and refund amount

Bago checks the payment, cancellation and collection history and may contact both the sender and traveller. If the conditions above are met, we confirm the approved refund amount and reference with you. Any amount already refunded or reversed is deducted so that the same payment is not refunded twice.

If your booking included third-party insurance, we also review the insurance payment and any issued cover with the insurance provider, and explain how it is treated in the refund confirmation.

How refunds are paid and tracked

Approved refunds are submitted through the payment provider to the original payment method used for the booking. We do not ask you to make another payment to receive a refund.

The time before funds appear depends on the payment provider, payment method and your bank. We provide the refund reference and the available processing estimate when the refund is submitted. If it has not arrived by that estimate, contact us with your booking and refund references so we can trace it.
